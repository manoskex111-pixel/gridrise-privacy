Privacy Policy

GRIDRISE: Racing Career

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Who We Are

GRIDRISE: Racing Career is an Android racing and career-management game provided by MKgames. For privacy questions or requests, contact manoskex111@gmail.com.

2. Game Data

The game stores gameplay progress and preferences, such as career saves, results, coins, upgrades, unlocks, and settings, on the device. This information is used to maintain your game progress and experience. Clearing the game's app data or uninstalling the game may delete locally saved progress.

3. Advertising and Data Collected by Third Parties

The game integrates Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID where available), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device and app information, ad views and interactions, and technical or diagnostic data. Such data may be used to serve and measure ads, detect fraud, maintain security, and personalize advertising where permitted. MKgames does not independently control the collection or retention practices of Google or its advertising partners.

4. How Data Is Used

Gameplay information is used to provide and maintain game functionality. Ad-related information may be used by Google and its partners for ad delivery, frequency control, reporting, fraud prevention, and advertising measurement. Watching rewarded advertisements is optional, but declining to watch means you do not receive the associated optional reward.

5. Sharing

The game uses Google AdMob, through which ad-related information may be shared with or processed by Google and its advertising partners. Locally stored gameplay progress is not intentionally uploaded to MKgames servers by the game as currently described. Other SDKs or services, if subsequently added, will require an update to this policy.

6. Privacy Choices and Rights

Where required by applicable law, users should be offered choices concerning advertising consent and personalization. You can manage ad privacy settings on your Android device and through Google's controls. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, deletion, correction, or restriction of personal data. Contact manoskex111@gmail.com with a privacy request. For data processed independently by Google, Google's privacy tools and policies also apply.

7. Retention and Deletion

Game progress is stored locally until it is overwritten, reset, or deleted using available device or game controls. You can remove local game data using Android's app-data controls; uninstalling may also remove it. MKgames cannot necessarily restore deleted local saves. Google determines retention periods for the advertising data it processes under its own policies. Contact MKgames for requests relating to any personal information under its control.

8. Data Security

MKgames aims to limit the information it handles and use the platform's normal security protections. Advertising data transmitted by third-party services is subject to those services' security practices. No electronic system is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

GRIDRISE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an MKgames account. Advertising shown to users, including any children within the game's declared audience, must comply with relevant child-privacy requirements and the applicable Google Play and AdMob settings. Parents or guardians may contact us with concerns. The game's declared target audience and ad configuration should be checked before release.
